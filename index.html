Want a note on a photo? Tap the photo, type a title or comment, then tap Done. It prints under the photo.

How it works

  1. Tap Choose photos and select all the photos from the job.
  2. Wait a moment while the photos get smaller.
  3. Tap Send, then tap Gmail. Do this for each email.

Photos are grouped so each email stays under Gmail's size limit. They arrive as one PDF per email, one photo per page.
